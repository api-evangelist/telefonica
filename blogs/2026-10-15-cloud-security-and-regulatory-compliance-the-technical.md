The cloud has become the technological foundation on which public and private organizations deploy new digital capabilities. However, cloud adoption goes far beyond simply moving workloads to new environments. Issues such as security, data management, regulatory compliance, and integration with artificial intelligence are redefining the way technology infrastructures are designed and operated.
