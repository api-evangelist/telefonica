Telefónica welcomes the Commission’s recognition that Europe’s future competitiveness, economic security and resilience depend on advanced, sustainable and high-quality digital infrastructures. The future Digital Networks Act should not be conceived as a new regulatory layer for European network operators. On the contrary, it should set the ground for a simpler, lighter and more forward-looking framework able to remove obsolete regulatory constraints and improve investment incentives.
