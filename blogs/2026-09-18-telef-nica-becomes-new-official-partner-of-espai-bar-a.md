·Telefónica strengthens its commitment to sport with a partnership agreement that makes it an official partner of the Spotify Camp Nou for the next five years. ·Telefónica España President Borja Ochoa and FC Barcelona President Joan Laporta have signed a partnership agreement between the two organisations. ·Telefónica will name one of the Lounge spaces being built in the stadium's double ring, accessible from the private boxes.
