In an environment characterised by digital transformation, artificial intelligence and constant change, people-centred leadership has become one of the key competitive advantages for organisations. Beyond simply managing teams, today’s leaders must drive talent development, foster trust, accelerate learning and create cultures where knowledge is shared naturally. In this context, mentoring emerges as one of the most powerful tools for strengthening leadership, preserving knowledge and preparing organisations to face the challenges of the future.
