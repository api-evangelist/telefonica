High-performing teams are not those that avoid conflict, but those that know how to manage it constructively. Healthy conflict allows different ideas, risks and perspectives to be brought to the table, thereby improving decision-making, strengthening trust and generating greater value for the organisation. To achieve this, leadership must foster psychological safety, open dialogue and a culture in which disagreeing is seen as a contribution rather than a threat.
