Leadership in today’s audiovisual industry requires a combination of adaptability, creativity, data analysis and the ability to take risks. In an environment characterised by the rise of streaming, audience fragmentation and the constant evolution of viewing habits, success depends on maintaining an authentic connection with the community, driving innovation and building teams committed to the content they produce. Passion, flexibility and a deep understanding of the audience remain key differentiators for successful leadership in the audiovisual sector.
