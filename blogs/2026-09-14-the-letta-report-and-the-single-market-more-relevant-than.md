In April 2024, former Italian Prime Minister Enrico Letta presented a report on the future of the EU single market. It offered an uncomfortable assessment of strategically important sectors, particularly telecommunications. Specifically, he warned that Europe is stuck in 27 small markets instead of growing into a strong union.
