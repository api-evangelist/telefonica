A memorable event is not one that boasts the most speakers, activities or budget. It is one that succeeds in evoking emotions, sparking conversation and building trust. Drawing on real-life experiences of organising events for clients, partners and commercial networks, this article analyses the key factors for designing events that enhance brand perception, strengthen business relationships and leave a lasting impact on attendees, clients and organisations.
