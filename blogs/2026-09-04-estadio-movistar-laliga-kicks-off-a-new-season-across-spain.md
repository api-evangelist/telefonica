· After attracting nearly 300,000 attendees across its four editions, Estadio Movistar LALIGA kicks off a new season by introducing outdoor activations that bring the passion of football beyond the stores and closer to fans. · This weekend, Málaga will host the launch of the new edition, combining open-air activities with experiences at the city’s Movistar store, including player meet-and-greets, skills challenges, prize draws and giveaways for attendees. · Visitors will have the chance to enter a draw to win tickets to a Málaga CF match and a jersey signed by the entire team.
