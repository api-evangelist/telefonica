Billing in an industry like telecommunications is no longer a purely administrative task; it has become a strategic element of the customer experience. For large clients, a bill, an inquiry, or an issue can influence the trust placed in the company and the long-term business relationship. José Antonio Piqueras explains why leadership in billing requires coordinating areas such as finance, sales, operations, customer service, and legal counsel, as well as anticipating problems and communicating complex information clearly.
